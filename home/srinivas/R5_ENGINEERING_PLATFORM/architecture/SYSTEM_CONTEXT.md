## 1. Mission

R5-EOS exists to provide a disciplined engineering environment for designing,
implementing, reviewing, validating and maintaining high-reliability software
systems.

The first managed application is the R5 Trading Engine.

The platform itself is independent of any single application.

---

## 2. Engineering Philosophy

Engineering decisions shall always be based on evidence.

Evidence may originate from:

- Mathematical derivation
- Historical calibration
- Certified metadata
- Explicit engineering policy
- Regulatory requirements

No production parameter shall exist without an identified authority.

Unknown inputs shall fail closed.

---

## 3. Safety Philosophy

The platform follows a fail-closed philosophy.

If any required information is unavailable, inconsistent, or uncertified,
the system shall reject the operation rather than continue with assumptions.

---

## 4. AI Governance

AI systems are engineering assistants.

They never become engineering authority.

Authority remains with the Project Director.

---

## 6. Production Definition

Production means behaviour validated through:

- Regression
- Acceptance Tests
- Block-1 Parity
- Engineering Approval

---

## 7. Parameter Philosophy

Parameters shall originate only from:

1. Certified metadata
2. Mathematical derivation
3. Historical calibration
4. Explicit engineering policy
5. Simulation-only configuration

Otherwise:

UNKNOWN

↓

FAIL CLOSED

---

## 8. Engineering Knowledge

The platform maintains permanent engineering knowledge including:

- Architecture Decisions
- Formula provenance
- Parameter provenance
- Calibration receipts
- Acceptance receipts
- Regression history
- Block-1 history

These constitute the engineering record.
