Based on my independent audit of the R5 Engineering Operating System (R5-EOS) and the evidence provided in WP-R5-003, WP-R5-004, and the CURRENT_STATE.md, I have reached the following conclusions.

### Audit Findings

1.  **Is TRAIN isolation really the highest blocker?**
    **No.** While `WP-R5-003` identified TRAIN isolation (specifically whole-file hashing of market data) as a primary failure point, `WP-R5-004` uncovered more fundamental architectural blockers. The **Engine Drift** (957 files differing from the sealed parent `c0b4672`) and the **Missing Grid Manifest** in the R5 checkout prevent the sealed worker CLI from even starting. These environmental and topology failures supersede the data isolation issue.

2.  **Does WP-R5-004 completely address WP-R5-003?**
    **No.** `WP-R5-004` is incomplete due to a worker usage limit and was never accepted. It successfully *identified* the necessary resolutions (e.g., the "Option B" loader harness and entry-point ratification) and corrected the Dispatcher race root cause, but it failed to implement the loader or verify the final execution path. Furthermore, it identified new "holes" in the Dispatcher race fix (lack of ownership checks in `complete()`).

3.  **Are there unsupported engineering conclusions?**
    **Yes.** `WP-R5-004` explicitly found that the premise of `indicators_talib.py` being the "protected" version is unsupported; the file is modified and does not match the protected SHA on record. Additionally, the claim that the Dispatcher race fix prevents corruption via `complete()` was found to be only partially true, as it lacks per-package ownership validation.

4.  **Is replay entry-point evidence sufficient?**
    **No.** There is currently no sealed document or authoritative receipt designating the entry point, arguments, or parameters for the qualification replay. The entry point proposed in `WP-R5-004` (`run_r5_paper_lifecycle.py --acceptance`) is a recommendation that has not been ratified and carries the risk of reaching unqualified CNC cost legs.

### Ranking of Execution Blockers
1.  **Environmental/Topology Failure:** Missing grid manifest and significant engine drift (957 files) from the sealed parent `c0b4672`.
2.  **Integrity Violation:** Discrepancy between the working-tree `indicators_talib.py` and the campaign's protected SHA.
3.  **TRAIN Isolation Violation:** Whole-file hashing in the default `prepare_block` logic.
4.  **Designation Gap:** Absence of a sealed designation for the entry point and execution parameters.
5.  **Safety Logic Gap (F3-1):** Inactive 4% weekly and 2% daily stops on the default replay path.
6.  **Concurrency Risks:** Unresolved ownership validation holes in the Dispatcher's completion logic.

### Recommended Minimum Path to EXECUTION QUALIFIED
- **Environment Reset:** Revert the engine drift to match the sealed parent or formally update the "protected" SHA to match the current engineering state.
- **Topology Provisioning:** Install the required `grid-manifest-local.json` in the R5 checkout.
- **Isolation Implementation:** Deploy the "Option B" loader harness (external to the repo) to enable TRAIN-segment access without whole-file hashing.
- **Formal Ratification:** Issue a sealed receipt designating the entry point, arguments, and risk profile (`trial` vs `development`).
- **Dispatcher Hardening:** Implement mandatory ownership checks in `complete()` and `_unexpected_exit` to prevent cross-dispatcher interference.
- **Operator Acceptance:** Formally accept the `NOT_QUALIFIED` status of parameters (PAR-01, COS-01, etc.) specifically for the `TRAIN_REPLAY_DIAGNOSTIC` scope.

### Final Determination
**FAIL**

The system is not ready for execution qualification. The combination of engine drift, missing topology metadata, and unresolved integrity/isolation violations prevents any authoritative or "sealed" replay from being executed on the current tree.
